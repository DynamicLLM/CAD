# Material Placeholders

Use these only as placeholders until the user provides real material data or company standards.

| Material | Typical use | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mild steel | general stamped parts | good default for examples |
| Stainless steel | corrosion-resistant stamped parts | stronger, heavier, usually higher forming force |
| Aluminum | lightweight brackets and panels | lower density, may require larger radii/thicker features |
| Brass/copper | electrical contacts and terminals | material cost and scrap utilization are important |
| Plastic | molded or machined parts | not normally progressive-die sheet-metal stock |

## Required Real Data

For real engineering, replace placeholders with:

- sheet thickness
- yield strength
- tensile strength
- shear strength
- bend radius limits
- elongation/formability
- grain direction
- coating/plating requirements
